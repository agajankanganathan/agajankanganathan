Sero

A brand identity and app/web UI for Sero, an AI customer-intelligence platform for independent cafés. It shows owners where they’re losing customers, and how to win them back.

Warm, calm and confident

A café-inspired palette of mahogany, chocolate and linen, set in Articulat CF, a modern take on Swiss typography. The symbol works on its own, beside the wordmark, and as an app icon in light, dark and alternative versions.

“Find why your cafe customers leave and win them back.”

A responsive site in light and dark themes, built around one idea: all your café data in one dashboard. It introduces Sero’s six modules in one platform.

Interactive prototypeScroll inside the screens, flip the light/dark switch, open the menu and tap any module.

Six modules, one platform

Each module solves one problem independent cafés face, and they all feed the same dashboard. Tap “Try it” to open that module in the working prototype.

01

AI Customer Insights

The problem
Most cafés only find out why a customer stopped coming when they read a bad review, if they find out at all.
What it does
Reads reviews, visit patterns and feedback together, then ranks the reasons guests don’t come back and suggests a fix for each.
In the dashboard
A ranked “why customers leave” list with an AI insight for each reason, plus the customer journey from first visit to regular.
02

Smart Promotions

The problem
Blanket discounts give money away to people who would have come in anyway.
What it does
Suggests targeted offers for specific groups, like guests who haven’t visited in 30 days, or triggers like a rainy morning.
In the dashboard
Each offer shows who it reaches and its projected weekly lift. Switch it on or off in one tap and the totals update.
03

Loyalty & Rewards

The problem
Paper stamp cards can’t tell you who your regulars are, or when they stop coming.
What it does
A digital stamp card and member list that flags regulars who are drifting away, so you can win them back early.
In the dashboard
Member stats, a live stamp card and an “at risk” list with a one-tap Send reward.
04

Menu Management

The problem
On a till report, best-sellers and money-losers look the same.
What it does
Tracks every item’s sales, margin and trend, and lets staff mark items sold out in seconds.
In the dashboard
A menu table with on/off switches for each item, and an AI tip on what to feature or re-price.
05

AI-generated response

The problem
Replying to every review takes time owners don’t have, so most go unanswered.
What it does
Drafts an on-brand reply to each review in the tone you choose, ready to edit and post.
In the dashboard
A review inbox: pick a review, choose Warm, Professional or Short, regenerate, then post.
06

Analytics Dashboard

The problem
Sales, reviews and loyalty data live in different apps that don’t talk to each other.
What it does
Brings every module into one home screen: revenue, customers, return rate and revenue at risk.
In the dashboard
Weekly or monthly views, a tap-to-read revenue chart, and live shortcuts into the other five modules.
